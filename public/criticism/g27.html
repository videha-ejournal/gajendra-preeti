The direction of a dictionary matters

The sampled opening includes agricultural vocabulary and a cluster of meanings associated with ankhua, while later entries move among investigation, searching and related actions. Beginning with Maithili words changes the work's cultural function: it lets a reader encounter a local vocabulary before deciding how English should explain or approximate it.

This direction should not be understood as a mechanically reversed English thesaurus. A local term may require an explanation, a phrase or a contextual example rather than a single English equivalent. The resource's critical value therefore depends on distinctions among senses and on the preservation of the source word's range. The note assesses selected entries, not every equivalence. Where extracted text is irregular, visual checking is necessary before attributing an error to the edition itself.
