Colour records the animals' movement

The inspected sequence uses a dog, a cat, spilled paint and trails of colour to carry action across largely open pages. Sparse sounds punctuate the images rather than explain everything they show. The encounter produces marks that remain visible, so movement becomes something the reader can trace after it has happened.

That economy gives the pictures substantial narrative work. Instead of checking a caption against an illustration, a child can infer cause and consequence from the changing trails and mixtures. The Maithili sounds belong to Preeti Thakur's translation, while the source credits Priya Kuriyan for writing and illustration. The source's English title is Bow Meow Wow; that wording is retained here. This note follows selected visual pages and does not claim that all possible interpretations of the sequence reduce to one lesson.
