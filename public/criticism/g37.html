The threshold between dream and waking

The publisher's visible contents group material around dream, awakening, time and recurring images of light, sky and thresholds. Those titles give the collection a recognisable field of imagery before a reader reaches the individual pieces. The opening verse section also suggests that formal boundaries are part of the book's presentation.

A contents list can establish those patterns, but it cannot show whether repetition develops an image or merely repeats it. That is the main critical question for a full reading: does a later return to light or sky revise the earlier meaning? The English title used here is a reading aid for the Maithili title, not a claim that the listed edition is wholly in English. This introduction is limited to publisher material and makes no invented account of its plots, speakers or conclusions.

A QUESTION TO CARRY BACK

When the same image returns in another section, what new experience would make it mean something different?
