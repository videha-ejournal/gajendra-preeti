If the viewer fails, can the source survive?

The published player provides presentation viewing alongside download routes, including GitHub and Archive resources. Its instructions also describe the use of Microsoft's online viewing service and the different handling of local files. These alternatives are valuable because a literary teaching resource is easier to preserve when viewing is not tied to only one route.

The trade-off is a dependency chain that the reader may not see at first. An online viewer requires connectivity and an external service; a downloaded presentation requires compatible software. Clear explanations of those conditions belong to the editorial design of access, not merely to technical support. This assessment concerns the page's exposed structure and instructions. It does not claim successful playback on every device or review every slide in the underlying course.
