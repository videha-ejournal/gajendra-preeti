An English collection needs its own entry points

The publisher identifies this as an English translation collection, with the linked listing beginning at Part 1. That makes the edition a potential bridge for readers who do not read Maithili, including those encountering the source literary world for the first time. The substantial volume format also makes navigation a central editorial concern.

The available description is too brief to support judgements about individual plots, prose or translation accuracy. This critical introduction therefore concentrates on the collection's stated purpose. A useful English edition needs clear individual titles, boundaries and contextual support without explaining away the source culture. The relevant test is not simply whether the words are English, but whether a new reader can enter one novel and follow its particular voice. Further assessment requires reading the translations themselves, not extrapolating from the collection's size.
