An author translates a narrative architecture

The publisher's master index explicitly preserves the sequence from chapter minus one hundred through chapter two hundred. Its early headings connect philosophical questions, memory and submerged records with social conflict. This suggests that the English edition carries the original's unusual relation to prehistory into its own structure, rather than replacing it with a conventional opening summary.

The index cannot demonstrate how the prose works in English. Author translation also poses a distinctive question: a change may be a translation decision, a revision, or both. A strong assessment would compare a complete scene across the two languages, attending to voice, explanatory additions and rhythm. This introduction is based on the publisher's chapter index and catalogue links to the volumes; it is not a full review of the English novel and should not be substituted for one.
