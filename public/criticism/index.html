HOW TO READ THIS LIBRARY

A reading, with its evidence in view.

These are English critical notes, not translations of the books. Some examine passages or images directly; others are explicitly marked introductions based on publisher material. Each entry states its basis and links to the source. English provides a shared critical reading layer for readers beyond Maithili; original titles and source-language works remain linked. The 41-language tool offers machine translation, not a separately reviewed edition.

Coverage, numbering, review status and limits

P-identifiers preserve positions in the original mixed catalogue used to assemble this library. Gaps such as P02, P04 and P06 do not indicate missing reviews or reserved forthcoming pages. All 17 Preeti entries in the consulted selection are present; the stable identifiers are retained for citations.

These notes were prepared with AI assistance and source/automated checks. No named human editorial review is recorded. Publisher- and catalogue-based introductions are starting points for reading, not substitutes for a full-text assessment.

The Preeti section follows the 17 items attributed to her in the public Videha catalogue, including translations and a manuscript archive. It is not a claim that no other work exists. Nine originally Drive-hosted PDFs are also preserved in this GitHub repository, with their source links and checksums retained. The combined Panji PDF is an existing Archive.org resource (840 MB); its full contents have not been compared against every Drive volume. No third-party host is a guarantee of permanent availability. The Gajendra section covers the 42 entries listed under “00 मुख्य समग्र” in the Samagra snapshot consulted on 8 September 2026.

Collected works and multi-volume series receive one assessment per catalogue entry, with their volume links retained. The three digital companions are assessed as resources, not mislabelled as books. These pages do not claim a separate close reading of every constituent volume or all thirty-seven children's novels.

English title renderings help navigation and may not be official translated titles. Original titles are retained. Translation, editing, adaptation and archival roles are distinguished; the original contributors' credits remain in the linked editions. All opinions here belong to these editorial notes, not to the featured authors.
