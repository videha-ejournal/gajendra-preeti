Where should a child begin?

The main Samagra catalogue groups thirty-seven children's novels and supplies multiple print-volume routes alongside a combined digital resource. That packaging makes a substantial body of writing discoverable in one place. It also raises an editorial question: a collection organised by number does not necessarily tell a child, parent or teacher where to begin.

The most useful critical framework here concerns entry points and progression. Individual novel titles, intended reading demands and links to separate texts would make the collected edition easier to navigate without reducing its range. This is a catalogue-based introduction, not a judgement of the thirty-seven plots or their prose. The combined PDF was not examined for this note, so claims about narrative quality, age suitability or the relationship between individual novels would exceed the evidence. The linked volumes preserve routes for that further reading.
