The doorway is an editorial choice

The linked gateway offers routes into the Gohi teaching frames, the retained presentation player and the English Water Burial material. Bringing these formats together reduces the need to discover each resource separately. At the same time, a landing page establishes a reading hierarchy through the route it presents first and the distinctions its labels make visible.

This is criticism of the published interface structure, not of a new standalone book. Its main task is orientation: readers should know whether a route leads to source narrative, a teaching interpretation or a documentary presentation. Frame counts communicate scale but do not explain what knowledge or time an entry point requires. The gateway is most useful when its convenience preserves those differences. Playback, accessibility with assistive technology and the accuracy of every frame were not tested for this note.
