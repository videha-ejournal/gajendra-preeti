THE WHOLE CURRENT

A village keeps asking one question:
who gets to keep a name?

Across the complete numbered sequence, Garh Narikel becomes a testing ground for truth, memory and responsibility. Part 0 travels through philosophical disputation and a twelve-hundred-year prehistory; Part 1 brings the inherited ritual into bridge deaths, courts, screens, privacy and archives; Part 2 expands through elemental and bodily justice, caste, food, theatre and a long investigation of seals, phones, warehouses and records.

The crocodile changes form, but so does the village’s answer. Oral memory becomes leaf, manuscript, register, print, code, song, public hearing and children’s question. The guide below keeps plot summary separate from supplied commentary and thematic tags.

SOURCE METHOD

Summary, commentary and motif are kept distinct.

This guide was built from the complete supplied single-volume manuscript, not from the contents titles alone. Every numbered chapter was segmented from its full text. Where the manuscript supplies its own chapter synopsis, that synopsis anchors the note; otherwise the guide condenses the chapter’s opening and closing narrative movement after reading the complete chapter block. Explicit authorial commentary is displayed separately as “Why this chapter matters.”

Theme tags and recurring-person labels are navigation aids derived from the chapter text. They are not replacements for the book’s argument, and the nineteen arcs are reader-facing groupings layered beneath—never instead of—the formal Part structure.
