Source method

Each supplied DOCX places its Maithili half first and its English counterpart later. This companion respects that internal bilingual architecture. The synopses are not title-based: they expand the source unit's philosophical problem, pūrvapakṣa or objection, response, proof strategy, language/translation discussion and conclusion.

All 32 top-level units are displayed from the start; filters only narrow the complete set. Eight substantial Sanskrit-translation annexures are separately identified and retain detailed internal contents so readers can follow the translated text's argumentative progression.
