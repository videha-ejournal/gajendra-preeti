Nav Pankhi — New Wings

Gajendra Thakur · Stories for young readers · 129 PDF pages

The book and its concerns

The supplied contents list twenty teenage stories, ranging from displacement and migration to libraries, floods, science, mobile phones, trees and shared investigation. The opening story explores dimensions through a laboratory conversation: beings confined to a line cannot perceive the possibilities available from another dimension. The scene makes a limit of perception available to imaginative inquiry rather than presenting a scientific demonstration of time travel.

A later sampled passage describes a village exhibition recording flood levels, safe places, drainage routes and remembered experience. Preserving a damaged book becomes inseparable from understanding and using what it contains. The final story’s recovered keys lead the children toward observation, reconstruction and checking before belief. Together, these samples place young people in active relations with evidence, community memory and practical problem-solving.
