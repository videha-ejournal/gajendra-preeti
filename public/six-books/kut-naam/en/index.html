The book and its concerns

The supplied novel foregrounds names, networks and the traces left by changing identities. In the sampled central dialogue, Sunayana notices a repeated pattern; Abhayakanta distinguishes a changeable name from the habits and recurring mistakes that are harder to alter. Identification is consequently presented as an act of comparing behaviour, not simply accepting a label.

The ending joins memory of violence to the possibility of renewed, less visible danger. Nand Aruni looks toward apparently calm water with a changed understanding of what it can conceal. The question is whether a community will recognise the next trace, rather than whether it already knows the next name. This reading is based on selected passages and the supplied contents; it does not claim to resolve every episode of the investigation. The source expressly identifies its characters and events as fictional.
