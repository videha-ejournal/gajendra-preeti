GAJENDRA THAKUR / TEN SOURCE EDITIONS

Sahasrashirsha

Gajendra Thakur · Novel · 338 PDF pages

The book and its concerns

Sahasrashirsha is organized through thirty-four kallols, or waves, each announced by a remembered line, song-fragment, proverb-like utterance or ritual/social image. The opening Purusha-sukta frame turns the novel toward multiplicity: many heads, many mouths, many accounts of truth and community.

The chapter discussions follow each kallol as a wave of village memory and social history. Rather than reducing the novel to plot alone, they foreground oral performance, caste and kinship markers, flood and agrarian life, domestic scenes, ritual language and the changing collective body of Mithila.
