The book and its concerns

The edition records first publication in 2020 and copyright years 2020 and 2026. Its contents present forty-two chapters, with disappearing people and documents, family secrets, arrest, bail and ownership of evidence among the named stages. These headings establish an investigative framework, but they should not be substituted for an account of how each event unfolds.

The sampled dialogue admits that memory may retain a detail from a book cover, clothing or childhood without its source being immediately recoverable. At the ending, Madhavi’s question about whether every witness needs a name receives an answer that not all witnesses testify in court. Physical materials remain separately preserved while an unresolved question survives. The closing image permits uncertainty without supplying a false answer: reason has a task and a reach, but completion of that task need not abolish every remaining question.

Chapter 1 — Seventeen minutes past four in the rain

अध्याय १ — बरखाक चारि बजि सतरह मिनट

Vasudha Karn is found dead inside the locked records room where she was examining discrepancies in the Seven Tola land case. A stopped clock, an unfinished sentence and Dulari’s disappearance encourage a premature story of suicide or flight. Madhavi’s knowledge of Vasudha’s routines makes the displaced betel box, missing cup and sugar residue newly significant. Retired investigator Jaynarayan Mandal asks that observations be recorded without forcing them into an immediate conclusion.

The chapter gives grief a place within inquiry without making emotional certainty evidence. Madhavi learns to distinguish clear recollection from a gap that distress might fill. Dulari must first be sought as a missing person rather than treated as a convenient suspect. The locked room becomes a contest between an apparently self-explanatory scene and the patient recovery of ordinary habits.
