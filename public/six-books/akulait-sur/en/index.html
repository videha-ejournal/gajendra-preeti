The book and its concerns

This updated edition brings 133 teenage ghazals together with 133 poem-song and illustrated child-reader units. The first division uses ghazal discipline—metre, rhyme-return, refrain and compact address—to handle play, study, digital pressure, friendship, time, work and self-trust. The second division shifts into poems and songs where trains, animals, sleep, rain, labour, national feeling and rural sound become performable scenes for young readers.

The individual discussions now treat each source unit by form and local cues: ghazal entries are read through their refrain and adolescent ethical situation; poem-song entries are read through sound, image, movement, call-and-response and illustration-aware performance. This replaces a generic summary with a map of how the book moves between ghazal, kavita, geet and child-facing visual imagination.
