# Preeti Thakur — children’s corpus provenance

Confirmed by the author/editor for the Videha Literary Atlas.

- 4 original Maithili children’s works authored by Preeti Thakur: `p0`, `p13`, `p14`, `p15`.
- 12 translated picture-books, `p1`–`p12`: English → Maithili, translated by Preeti Thakur.
- `विद्यापतिक पुरुष परीक्षा` (`p15`) is an original Maithili children’s work by Preeti Thakur; it must not be described as an adaptation.

This note is intentionally separate from Gajendra Thakur’s translation corpora and from his 37 original-Maithili children’s novels / graphic novels translated into English by himself.
