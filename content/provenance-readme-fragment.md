## Author/translation corpus distinctions

The atlas keeps these confirmed families separate:

1. Preeti Thakur: 4 original Maithili children’s works authored by her; 12 picture-book translations from English into Maithili by her.
2. Gajendra Thakur: 37 original Maithili children’s novels / graphic novels authored by him and translated into English by him.
3. Gajendra Thakur: 45 Maithili translation entries; items 1–3 use Indian-language originals via English intermediary, items 4–45 are translated directly from English into Maithili, all by Gajendra Thakur.
4. Sanskrit classics/philosophy: 13 Sanskrit drama/classics + 4 Sanskrit philosophical works translated Sanskrit → Maithili → English, both translation stages by Gajendra Thakur.
