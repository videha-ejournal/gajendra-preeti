# Gajendra Thakur — Maithili translation corpus provenance

Confirmed by the author/editor for the Videha Literary Atlas.

- Entries 1–3: originals in various Indian languages → English intermediary → Maithili translation by Gajendra Thakur.
- Entries 4–45: English → Maithili translation by Gajendra Thakur.
- Entry 46, `मू परियोजना — देवनागरी लिपि`, is intentionally not classified in this 45-entry Maithili translation corpus because the supplied listing labels it `Gajendra Thakur — English`, not `अनुवाद (मैथिली)`.

The exact 45 titles and URLs are stored in `content/gajendra-maithili-translations-provenance.json`.
